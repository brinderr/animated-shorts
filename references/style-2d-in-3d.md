# Built-in style: 2D characters in a 3D world

Measured targets (adapted from mromidev/claude-animated-shorts, MIT, study of 20 viral shorts in this style).

## Format
- Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, 45-78 s (median ~60 s). Voice starts on frame 1, no intro, no logo, no outro.
- One narrator, 3.5-4.1 words per second, almost no pauses. Music/SFX 5-10 dB under the voice. -14 LUFS.

## Picture
- Characters: flat 2D cards, simple faces, plum-dark outline (#201628 to #2f1d28), about 3.5 head-heights tall, bean head (height/width ~0.93), stick limbs (~0.055 of head width), dot hands, no blush, no eye highlights.
- World: real 3D, hand-painted looking textures, saturated colours, near-black ink lines (~3 px at 1080 wide) on silhouettes, depth jumps and sharp creases. No line on the horizon or floor joins.
- Colour: day median brightness ~0.57, no crushed blacks (darkest ~0.23 outside ink), strong saturation. Warm highlights, cool blue or violet shadows, never brown shadows. Sky top ~#5e93d9.
- Light follows the story: noon for setup, golden for drama, deep night blue for the explanation.
- Depth: lens blur on about one third of screen time (close-ups, medium shots), 10-16 px at 1080 wide, focus on the eyes. Fog = horizon colour.

## Camera
- 0-1 hard cuts per video. Transitions are camera moves: follow an object to the next subject, whip pan, fly through a window, or a 6-8 frame dissolve.
- Glides of 18-40 frames with smooth in and out. Every hold creeps in slowly (0.15-0.35 % per frame). Low camera height, 4-5° dutch tilt for drama.
- Impact shake flips direction every frame and decays over 7 frames.

## Effects
Flat 2D sprites placed in the 3D scene: dust puffs, sparkles, speed lines, splashes, music notes, light shafts, a 4-frame white flash after the hook.

## Script
Paradox hook in sentence 1 → setting → 3-5 escalating beats with short dialogue → one mystery question → "You see," + everyday comparison → ironic payoff in 1-2 short sentences. Sentences ~13 words, plain spoken English, contractions.
